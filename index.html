› Welcome

This is a collaborative group project conducted by Iliana Carlos, Ariana Vargas, and Lewis Vidana. We interviewed Sam Sermerno (they/she), an archivist at the Outwords Archive. It is an oral history project and digital archive dedicated to capturing, preserving, and sharing the stories of LGBTQIA2S+ elders, to build community and catalyze social change.

The role involves admin email project coordination, recurring staff meetings including training and workshops for queer archives coalition, and the bulk of the archival work which focuses on digital preservation. The latter responsibility involves a lot of back end metadata tagging, encoding, researching and indexing of our oral history transcripts.
